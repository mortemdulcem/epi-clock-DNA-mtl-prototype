1. UNODC Tarzı + DNA Yazılımı: Genel Görünüm
UNODC tarzından gelenler:

Üstte ince, sade top bar, beyaz zemin, koyu mavi vurgu.
Çok temiz, bol beyaz alan, sade ikonlar.
Kurumsal mavi tonları, nötr gri, az sayıda vurgu rengi.
DNA yazılımından gelenler:

Dashboard, tablolar, grafikler, sekmeli paneller.
Örnek listeleri, analiz kartları, kalite metrik grafikleri.

3. Kullanabileceğin Prompt’lar (UNODC + DNA Yazılımı)
Aşağıdaki prompt’ları DALL·E, Midjourney veya UI üreten araçlarda direkt kullanabilirsin. Hepsi UNODC görünümünü özellikle vurguluyor.

Prompt 1 – Genel Ana Sayfa (Dashboard)
"Design a homepage UI for a national scientific DNA analysis software, in the visual style of the United Nations Office on Drugs and Crime (UNODC).

The interface should be:

Clean, institutional, and minimalist, with a strong United Nations look.
Use a color palette similar to UNODC: deep UN blue, light blue accents, white backgrounds, and neutral greys.
Top navigation bar with logo on the left and menu items on the right: Dashboard, Samples, Analyses, Reports, Database, Settings.
A hero-like header section with the title 'National DNA Analysis System' and a short description, styled like an official UN program page.
Below, show:
Statistic cards (Total Samples, Ongoing Analyses, Completed Reports).
A table of recent analyses (Sample ID, Type, Status, Date).
Small charts for daily analysis counts and sample types.
Use clean sans-serif typography, subtle lines instead of heavy borders, and lots of white space. The overall look must clearly resemble a UNODC-like institutional website, but the content is for a DNA/genetics laboratory system."

Prompt 2 – Analiz Detay Sayfası (UNODC Tarzında)
"Create a detailed analysis view screen for a DNA software UI designed in the visual style of UNODC (United Nations Office on Drugs and Crime).

Layout:

Top UN-style header with the software name and breadcrumb navigation.
A clean content area with a big title 'NGS Analysis – Sample ID 2025-001'.
Tabs or horizontal navigation for: Overview, Quality Control, Alignment, Variants, STR Profile.
In the 'Overview' tab, show:
A summary panel with key metrics (coverage, read count, status).
A simple chart or two (bar or line charts) with UN-like blue color accents.
On the side, a narrow column with sample metadata (sample type, date, operator, lab).
Visual style:

White background, light grey cards, thin lines.
Deep blue headers and titles, small light blue accent elements.
Very clean, official, and formal, similar to a UN agency internal system."
Prompt 3 – UNODC Tarzında “Program / Modül Listesi” Ekranı
"Design a modules overview page for a National DNA Analysis System, visually inspired by the UNODC website.

Include:

A UN-style top navigation bar.
A main title: 'Modules and Programs'.
A grid of cards, each representing a module:
Forensic STR Analysis
NGS Variant Analysis
Population Genetics
Clinical Genomics
Reports and Legal Output
Data and Statistics
Each card should have:
A simple icon
A short description
A 'Open Module' button
Use UNODC-like design language: institutional blue tones, flat white backgrounds, light grey separators, and clean typography. No flashy gradients, just subtle, professional styling."

Prompt 4 – UNODC Kurumsal + DNA Veri / İstatistik Sayfası
"Create a 'Data and Statistics' page for a DNA analysis platform, with the same visual feeling as the UNODC Data and Research pages.

Elements:

Page header with title 'DNA Data and Statistics'.
A section of key indicators in small cards (e.g., number of samples analyzed, types of tests, distribution by year).
Clean charts: bar chart, line chart, maybe a simple world or country map showing sample origins.
Tab navigation for 'National Statistics', 'Laboratory Performance', 'Population Genetics Data'.
Visual style:

UNODC-inspired colors (deep blue, white, light greys).
Clear, simple chart styling with blue as the primary series color.
Institutional, serious, and data-driven appearance."
Prompt 5 – UNODC Gibi Giriş / Login Ekranı
"Design a login screen for a National DNA Analysis System that visually feels like a UNODC or United Nations secure portal.

Centered login card on a light background.
Logo and system name at the top: 'National DNA Analysis System'.
Input fields for username and password, and a 'Sign In' button in UN blue.
Optional small link 'Language: Türkçe | English'.
Very minimal, with a small abstract background illustration related to science or justice, in light blue tones.
The overall aesthetic must be clean, formal, and UN-style institutional."
